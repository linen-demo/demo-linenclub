FAQs

Can linen clothing from Linen Club be worn in all seasons?

Yes, linen clothing from Linen Club can be worn throughout the year because its fabric is breathable. In summer, it will keep you cool, while its lightweight layering options will keep you comfortable during the cooler months.

What is pure linen?

Natural fabric that is fully made of flax fibres is called pure linen. It is breathable and eco-friendly. Linen Club ensures premium pure linen quality from the best of French and Belgian flax.

Is 100% linen better than cotton?

100% linen is more breathable, durable, and moisture-wicking compared to cotton. It is also eco-friendly and ideal for year-round comfort, making it a premium choice for clothing.

Is linen clothing suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes, linen is hypoallergenic, soft, and gentle on the skin, making it an excellent choice for people with sensitive skin. Linen Club's pure linen fabric ensures maximum comfort and minimal irritation.

Is Linen Club an Indian brand?

Yes, Linen Club is an Indian brand renowned for its premium linen collection, crafted with a focus on quality and innovative European technology.
